# PRODUCT.md

## Register

brand

## Users & Purpose

Arabic-first (MENA) homeowners evaluating Barosec Secure subscription plans. They want clear plan comparison, feature trust, and a path to subscribe — in Arabic RTL by default, with English available.

## Brand Personality

calm · confident · protective

## Anti-references

- Purple-on-white SaaS gradients
- Cream/paper editorial AI landing pages
- Dense mega-nav storefront clones
- Neon cyber-security aesthetics

## Design Principles

1. One job per section: hero, plans, features, benefits, FAQ.
2. Plan clarity over decoration — prices and feature differences must scan in seconds.
3. RTL-native: logical properties, Arabic typography first.
4. Light refresh of Barosec Secure structure — not a pixel clone, not a new product story.
